## climate.py
- 13 bases × delivered source lists (prcp/spi/spei: chirps, persiann, era5land; temperature: cpc, era5land; sm_z: era5land, gldas21; evi_anom: terra, aqua).
- Ensemble = shared `add_dynamic_ensemble` rule (R34): equal-weight mean of finite sources, n_sources, population SD (ddof 0) only n>=2, single source mean = source with SD missing, n=0 missing; never zero-filled.
- Provider outcome classes: `published` (NaN = legitimate per-area missingness) / `unavailable` (definitive); everything else (malformed table, non-numeric, inf, duplicate, missing roster area, unknown status, wrong source set) = `ClimateProcessingFailure` (blocks; never reclassified).
- Monthly update (R43/R41/R8): every unsaved month from the first saved month through O, oldest first; each indicator-month with >=1 published source committed immediately (rows: admin_code, month, value, n_sources, ens_sd, method_id), never rewritten even when a late provider arrives; month with no published source = recorded source-level gap; O with no source = `ClimateSourceMissing` (R13), earlier saved months remain.
- Empty store refused: history must be initialised from the accepted delivered history (R45) first.
- Seasons (R33): only windows with gs_end_date_exclusive <= first day of O+1 are complete; per (season_year, season) only the newly completed, unsaved areas are fetched and saved; never rewritten; unknown areas rejected.
- Roster (R32): published tables must cover every package-roster area exactly once; out-of-roster areas (e.g. the GEE assets' 6,227th admin) dropped and counted, never required.
- Method ids (R40): script path + sha256 of the shared-folder source, spatial weighting PIXEL_METRIC_THEN_PIXELAREA_WEIGHTED_ADMIN_MEAN, outlier ABS_Z_GT6_WARNING_ONLY_VALUE_KEPT, SPEI fit SPEI_UBPWM_GLO_STRICT_SUPPORT_V3, ensemble EQUAL_WEIGHT_VALID_SOURCES_POPSD_DDOF0_MIN2_V1; compact method_id on every saved row.
